I triaged 22 U-Boot CVEs against Toradex BSP 7 on the Verdin iMX8M Plus (u-boot-toradex SRCREV 3f772959 plus the recipe patches, verdin-imx8mp_defconfig): the 11 that NVD's CPE match returns for 2024.07, plus 11 published in Aug-Sep 2026 that NVD has not given CPE data yet, so cve-check does not report them. In the stock config 5 apply, all in U-Boot proper and none in SPL: CVE-2024-57256 (ext4 symlink), CVE-2024-57258 (dlmalloc), CVE-2026-15390 and CVE-2026-71971 (IP fragment reassembly, only reachable while U-Boot is on the network) and CVE-2025-70293 (ext4 write path). Seven upstream commits fix all five and apply cleanly to the BSP 7.8.0 tree. Note that the fix NVD lists for 57256 (35f75d2a46e5) is not enough on 64-bit; you also need 107ed84602bb "ext4: Fix zalloc()". CVE-2026-46728 (FIT signature bypass) only matters with secure boot, and the Toradex backport (2ed62810) first ships in BSP 7.8.0, so signed-FIT builds on 7.0.0 to 7.7.0 are affected. The OpenVEX file, per-CVE evidence and limitations are here: https://github.com/Vaten0x/toradex-bsp7-uboot-vex
